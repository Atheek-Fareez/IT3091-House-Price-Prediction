# INDUSTRY EXPLORER TRACK

## Important: Dataset, Problem & Client Must All Line Up

Before you go further with your project, read this carefully. This is one of the most common ways groups lose marks on the Industry Explorer Track, and it is easy to avoid once you understand the order things are supposed to happen in.

### The three rules

1. The **dataset must match the problem**, not the other way around.
2. You **cannot invent a business problem** just to justify a dataset you happened to find online.
3. The **problem, the dataset, and the feedback** on your recommendation must all come from the **same real client or stakeholder**.

### Think of it in this order:

Find a real client with a real problem > understand what they actually need > collect or use data that is genuinely relevant to that problem, ideally from them > build your analysis and recommendation > go back to the client and get their feedback on whether it actually helps them.

| Not this                                                                                                                                            | This instead                                                                                                                                                 |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| "We found an interesting sales dataset online, so we decided our client is a retail store and made up a problem that fits the columns in the data." | "We spoke to a real business, understood a real problem they have, then collected or used data relevant to that specific problem, from them where possible." |

A dataset on its own does not prove anything. Markers will be checking whether your stakeholder, your problem, and your data all belong to the **same real situation**, not whether you found a dataset that looks impressive. If the data was not provided by or verified with your client, and the problem was invented afterward to match the columns available, this will be treated as not meeting the criteria below.

### 7. Industry Explorer Track Bonus Rubric

Industry Explorer groups follow the same 100-mark core rubric. They may receive up to 10 additional bonus marks only after approval and evidence review. The bonus is not awarded simply for finding a dataset online.

| Bonus Criterion                                                              | Marks | Evidence Required                                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Real stakeholder or business context identified                              | 2     | Named stakeholder/context, decision need, and realistic value.                            |
| Problem scope approved and suitable for ML/data mining                       | 2     | Approved early; manageable within seven weeks; not a full software/deep learning project. |
| Dataset is original, local, stakeholder-provided, or meaningfully real-world | 2     | Data source is verifiable; dataset fits the selected decision lens.                       |
| Data collection method, data dictionary, and privacy handling documented     | 2     | Permission/anonymisation where relevant; no unsafe personal/sensitive data handling.      |
| Final recommendation has practical business value                            | 2     | Evidence-based and meaningful for the stakeholder.                                        |
