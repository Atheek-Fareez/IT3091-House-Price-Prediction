# IT3091 Machine Learning – Assignment Descriptor

## IT3091 Machine Learning – Group Assignment Descriptor

*guided reasoning, business lenses, evidence-based ML decisions*

---

## 1. Overview: two assignment tracks

This assignment gives each group a platform to practise the capabilities required in the AI era: problem framing, critical thinking, data/statistical reasoning, ML implementation, responsible AI judgement, communication, collaboration, and learning how to learn. The dataset or business scenario is the starting point; the technical pathway must be reasoned and justified by the group.

| Track | What is provided / expected | What students must decide |
|---|---|---|
| Guided Data Track | The module team provides a business scenario and dataset/source. Students work within a controlled domain. | Primary lens, optional secondary lens, exact ML/data-mining task, target/output, preprocessing, models/methods, evaluation metric, and recommendation. |
| Industry Explorer Track | Students identify an approved real-world business problem and obtain/collect a suitable dataset. Prior approval is required. | Same technical requirements as Guided Data Track plus evidence of real-world engagement, ethical handling, and practical business value. Eligible for up to 10 bonus marks. |

---

## 2. Business lenses

Each group must choose one primary decision lens. One secondary lens is allowed only when it directly strengthens the primary decision. A secondary lens is optional; doing more work without coherence will not earn higher marks. Marks are awarded for defensible reasoning, not for attempting many disconnected analyses.

## 5. Main marking rubric: what this assignment encourages

| Criterion | Marks | High-mark evidence | Fink alignment | AI-era skill |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Business problem framing and lens/task formulation | 5 | Clear stakeholder, decision need, primary lens, optional secondary lens, unit of analysis, exact task/output, and rationale. | Foundational Knowledge; Integration | Problem framing; critical thinking |
| Workflow diagram and decision log | 10 | A coherent workflow from business problem to recommendation; important decisions recorded with options, reasons, and evidence. | Integration; Learning How to Learn | Reasoning; communication |
| Data understanding, EDA, and data quality reasoning | 10 | Data dictionary, row meaning, variable types, patterns, data issues, and explanation of how issues affect the task. | Foundational Knowledge; Application | Data literacy; statistical reasoning |
| Preprocessing and feature-engineering decisions | 15 | Justified handling of missing values, outliers, duplicates, categories, scaling, imbalance, leakage, and non-tabular features where relevant. | Application | ML implementation; data reasoning |
| Model/data-mining strategy and comparison | 20 | Sensible baseline and at least three alternatives; choice of methods justified by task, data, interpretability, and constraints. | Application; Integration | ML implementation; problem solving |
| Evaluation, validation, and critical judgement | 20 | Appropriate metrics, train/test or cross-validation logic, leakage prevention, honest interpretation, and comparison beyond accuracy. | Application; Integration | Statistical reasoning; responsible AI |
| Recommendation, limitations, and stakeholder value | 10 | Evidence-based practical recommendation, clear limitations, risks, and stakeholder value. | Integration; Human Dimension; Caring | Communication; human-centred AI |

## 7. Industry Explorer Track bonus rubric

Industry Explorer groups follow the same 100-mark core rubric. They may receive up to 10 additional bonus marks only after approval and evidence review. The bonus is not awarded simply for finding a dataset online.

| Bonus criterion | Marks | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Real stakeholder or business context identified | 2 | Named stakeholder/context, decision need, and realistic value. |
| Problem scope approved and suitable for ML/data mining | 2 | Problem approved early; manageable within seven weeks; not a full software/deep learning project. |
| Dataset is original, local, stakeholder-provided, or meaningfully real-world | 2 | Data source is verifiable; dataset fits the selected decision lens. |
| Data collection method, data dictionary, and privacy handling documented | 2 | Permission/anonymisation where relevant; no unsafe personal/sensitive data handling. |
| Final recommendation has practical business value | 2 | Recommendation is evidence-based and meaningful for the stakeholder. |

---

## 8. Final message

This assignment is not about following a fixed recipe. The highest marks will go to groups that make the most defensible machine learning/data mining decisions: clear problem framing, evidence-based data reasoning, justified preprocessing, suitable model/method comparison, honest evaluation, practical recommendation, responsible AI judgement, and clear individual learning.
